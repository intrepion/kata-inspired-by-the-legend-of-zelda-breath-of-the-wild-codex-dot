# Aster Vale

A wilderness whose three lost wind currents are restored by a traveling wayfarer.

## Language

**Wayfarer**: The traveler returning light to the valley.
_Avoid_: Hero, Link

**Wind beacon**: One of three ancient structures that holds a current of wind.
_Avoid_: Shrine, tower

**Aster shard**: A glowing mineral offered to the meadow beacon.
_Avoid_: Rupee, currency

**Sunberry**: A wild fruit that restores a wayfarer's strength.
_Avoid_: Potion, heart container

**Sentinel**: A stone defender whose strike is preceded by a visible warning.
_Avoid_: Guardian, monster

**Wind dial**: A rotating rune that aligns the highland beacon.
_Avoid_: Switch, code lock

**Heartspire**: The central ruin where the restored currents reunite.
_Avoid_: Castle, final boss

**Recovery**: A return to the last restored beacon after defeat, preserving discoveries.
_Avoid_: Game over, restart
