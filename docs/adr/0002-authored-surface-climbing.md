# 0002: One shared authored climbable surface

Status: accepted through user-delegated recommended defaults.

Replace the misleading sprint-slope-only climbing claim with one explicit vertical surface and a standable summit linked to existing gliding. Pure world geometry drives contact normals, collision, top height, collectible altitude, presentation and save recovery. This offers a testable bounded climb/traverse/drop/exhaust/top-out contract without adding a general mesh physics engine.

Keep the established save version and twelve shard IDs. Rehome one optional shard; prior collection remains valid. Transient contact is not persisted, and old/new positions inside the added solid are normalized to safe terrain outside it. This preserves campaign progress at the cost of re-climbing after a summit reload. Numbers and the cylindrical tower are original authored approximations, not a general Breath of the Wild recreation.
