# Aster Vale: acceptance specification

An original third-person wilderness adventure inspired by freedom, landmark navigation and systemic traversal. All geometry, text and synthesized sound are original. No Nintendo assets.

1. A readable varied low-poly valley with three distant beacon landmarks and a central finale, a following orbit camera, shadows, atmosphere and animated character.
2. Walk, stamina sprint, jump, climb steep slopes while sprinting and glide while airborne. Boundaries, obstacle collision and falling recovery must prevent losing the player.
3. Collect at least five shards for the meadow offering. Align three highland wind dials to the visible rune clue. Defeat three telegraphing sentinels for the eastern beacon. Objectives can be completed in any order; repeated interactions do not duplicate rewards.
4. Each restored beacon unlocks a recovery point. All three permit the Heartspire finale. Continue exploring after completion.
5. Health, stamina, healing with berries, visible attack feedback, enemy warnings and objective guidance. Accessible keyboard and touch controls, pause/help, sound toggle and explicit new journey control.
6. Persist discoveries, position, health and finale locally. Validate corrupted saves, handle storage failures, reload and interruption safely. Pause freezes the simulation.
7. Public gameplay command and save/load tests, type check and production build. Desktop and mobile visual inspection and actual start-to-finale play in the Mac browser, with coverage recorded honestly.

Target duration: 10–15 minutes of first-time exploration, not enforced by timers. This compact slice prioritizes coherent mechanics over a huge map.
